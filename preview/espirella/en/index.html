From bean to cup

  1. PHOTO: origin – coffee plantations
    01

    Origin

    Our coffee comes from several growing regions, for example Brazil and Colombia. The beans are picked by hand so that only the very best reach the roastery.

  2. PHOTO: roasting – freshly roasted beans
    02

    Roasting

    We buy from smaller independent growers who look after their plantations with great care. We roast to a medium level so the natural flavour comes through, and only then do we pack it.

  3. PHOTO: preparation – espresso from the machine
    03

    Preparation

    The final step to a perfect cup is preparation. Our coffees are made for espresso, but they taste great from a French press too – give it a try.
